About

A
Complete Vision

I'm Raphaël Laurelli. I work at the intersection of traditional cinematography and aerial filmmaking. I control the entire visual pipeline of a project: from stabilised capture and precision FPV piloting to editing and final colour grading.

This end-to-end approach allows me to maintain absolute image consistency. Whether shooting on the ground or in the air, I structure every shot with the edit and colour grade in mind — ensuring an optimal workflow and a cinematic aesthetic with a distinct identity.

Services

What I can do
for your project

I.
Immersive Property Tour

Fluid walk-throughs combining high-altitude aerial shots, stabilised ground footage and the magic of FPV fly-throughs. Seamless transitions from exterior to interior spaces. Ideal for high-end real estate and hospitality.

II.
Corporate Profile

On-camera interviews with refined lighting and isolated directional audio capture to eliminate background noise. Paired with fluid B-roll that illustrates the working environment. The perfect solution to strengthen the image and credibility of companies and craftspeople alike.

III.
Action & Adrenaline

High-speed chases and close-proximity tracking with FPV drones and action cameras. We capture the essence of movement through extreme perspectives and high-impact vertical or horizontal editing. Built for motorsport, extreme sports and live events.

IV.
Premium Promotional Film

The ultimate visual statement for your brand. A complete blend of ground and aerial cinematography, high-fidelity capture in professional formats and a cinematic colour grade. A bespoke, full narrative for the most demanding advertising campaigns.
