About

A
Complete Vision

I'm Raphaël Laurelli. I work at the intersection of traditional cinematography and aerial filmmaking. I control the entire visual pipeline of a project: from stabilised capture and precision FPV piloting to editing and final colour grading.

This end-to-end approach allows me to maintain absolute image consistency. Whether shooting on the ground or in the air, I structure every shot with the edit and colour grade in mind — ensuring an optimal workflow and a cinematic aesthetic with a distinct identity.
